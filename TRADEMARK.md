# OpenWP trademark and branding policy

The **code** of OpenWP is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Anyone can use, change, fork and redistribute it.

The **name and the identity** are not part of that license. "OpenWP", the OpenWP logo, "OpenWP Bridge", "OpenWP Master" and the address openwpmcp.com belong to AI Next Studio (https://ainextstudio.it). We keep them so that when you see "OpenWP" you know it is the project made and maintained by us.

## What you can do, without asking

- Install, use, study and modify OpenWP, including for paying clients.
- Redistribute the **unmodified** plugin and server with the name OpenWP, keeping the copyright notice in the [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE) files.
- Say, truthfully, that your product or tutorial "works with OpenWP" or "is compatible with OpenWP", and link to https://openwpmcp.com.
- Write articles, videos and reviews about OpenWP using its name and logo as shown in this repository.

## What you cannot do

- Publish a **modified** version under the name OpenWP, "OpenWP Bridge" or a confusingly similar name (for example "Open-WP", "OpenWP Pro", "OpenWP Plus"). A fork has to choose its own name and say it is "based on OpenWP by AI Next Studio".
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests that we make, approve or support your product when we do not.
- Sell OpenWP, or a service called OpenWP, as if it were your own product.
- Register OpenWP, or a similar name or logo, as a trademark, domain, app or store listing.

## Commercial services

Hosted, paid or enterprise services built around OpenWP are separate products from AI Next Studio and are not part of this repository. Nothing that is published here under the MIT License is taken back: the versions already released stay MIT.
